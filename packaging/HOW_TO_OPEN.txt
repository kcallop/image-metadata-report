PHOTO DETAILS REPORT
====================

Turns a photo into a readable PDF: a preview, plain-language camera and
settings info, location (if any), and a full technical appendix.

INSTALL
1. Drag "Photo Details Report" onto the Applications folder.
2. Eject this disk image.

FIRST LAUNCH (one time only)
This app is not signed by Apple, so macOS will warn you the first time.

  1. Open your Applications folder.
  2. RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) "Photo Details Report" and choose Open.
  3. In the dialog that appears, click Open.

If you only see "Done" and "Move to Bin" (newer macOS versions):
  1. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the message about "Photo Details Report" and click
     "Open Anyway", then confirm.

After that, it opens normally with a double-click.

USING IT
Click "Choose a photo...", pick a file (JPEG, HEIC, PNG, RAW such as NEF,
CR2, ARW, DNG, and others), choose where to save the PDF, and it opens
automatically.

PRIVACY
Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. Note that photos can
contain GPS location - the report warns you when this is the case.

REQUIREMENTS
A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer.
